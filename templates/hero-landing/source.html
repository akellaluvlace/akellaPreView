Pre-flight checks

Questions teams ask in week zero.

01

Will Lumen replace our existing tools?

No. Lumen sits next to Linear, Jira, Notion, GitHub and Slack. It indexes them, draws on them, and makes the seams disappear. Most teams keep every tool they had — they just stop tab-hopping.

02

How long does the trial run?

14 days. No card, no calls. If you ship one release note in week one, you'll know whether it works for your team.

03

Where is our data hosted?

EU and US regions, your choice at workspace creation. SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, and GDPR-aligned. Customer-managed keys (CMK) on Pro and above.

04

Does the AI train on our content?

No. Drafts are generated per-workspace and discarded after rendering. We never use your content as training data. The full data-handling brief is in the security centre.

05

Can we self-host?

Yes — single-tenant on AWS, GCP, or Azure on the Enterprise plan. We help you wire SSO, audit log forwarding, and the changelog connector.
