The Method

Five movements that constitute every engagement.

The method has been refined across thirty-eight engagements. Each movement is iterative, evidence-led, and unsigned — we do not ship a slide deck and a handshake. We ship a structural intervention you can audit.

"The instinct to act precedes the obligation to understand. We invert that order, on principle." — E. Thorne, Founding Note · 2014
  1. I

    Diagnostic Listening

    Six weeks of unscripted interviews across three organisational layers. We refuse to read the org chart until we have read the room.

    Wks 01–06
  2. II

    Network Mapping

    We graph the informal communication topology against the formal hierarchy. The delta — almost always large — is where attrition lives.

    Wks 04–10
  3. III

    Hypothesis Drafting

    We commit to three falsifiable claims about the organisation. Each one carries its own measurement protocol and its own kill criteria.

    Wks 09–14
  4. IV

    Structural Intervention

    A single, surgical change. Drafted with the executive team, executed by the executive team, monitored against the kill criteria from movement III.

    Wks 14–22
  5. V

    Withdrawal & Documentation

    We leave a closed-form report and a maintenance protocol. We do not retain the engagement past month nine. The institution is not a client of ours indefinitely.

    Wks 22–26
