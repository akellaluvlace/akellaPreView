Common Questions

If we haven't answered yours, write to us. The coaches read every message.

What's "intermediate"?

add

A bodyweight squat for sets of five, two years of consistent training, no obvious form holes on video. We'll send a 4-question intake before you sign up — if you're not ready, we say so.

What if I miss a session?

add

One a week is allowed without changing the cycle. More than that, your coach restructures the week and shifts the realization phase. Tell us early; we'll keep the block honest.

Do I need a barbell at home?

add

For Foundation, Hypertrophy, and Strength: a barbell, plates to ~150% bodyweight, a rack, and a bench. For Peak: meet-spec only — competition bar, calibrated plates. We can sub equipment on request, but the block is built around the standards.

Is nutrition included?

add

Strategy, yes. Macro tracking, no. Each cycle includes a one-page nutrition reference for the phase you're in (surplus / lean / cut), plus a brief on supplements. We don't write daily meal plans; that's a different job.

Refund window?

add

Two weeks, full. After that, prorated to the week of cancellation. Nobody is forced to finish a block; we'd rather you came back than slogged through.
