Detail · 02 — Architecture

How a request routes through the edge.

Every inference call traverses four stages — ingestion, routing, runtime warm-up, and execution — orchestrated by a Rust control plane that never leaves the data path.

  1. 01

    Ingestion

    A request arrives at the nearest of 12 anycast edges. TLS terminates locally; auth is verified against a signed JWT cached in memory.

    avg · 0.8ms
  2. 02

    Router

    A consistent-hash router pins the request to a node already holding the model weights, avoiding a transfer hop.

    avg · 1.4ms
  3. 03

    Cold-start mitigation

    If the chosen node has no warm runtime, a snapshot-restore primitive resurrects state in <5ms — never a 300ms container boot.

    p99 · 4.6ms
  4. 04

    Inference

    Tensor-parallel CUDA kernels stream tokens back through the same socket. First-token latency lands inside our SLA budget on every call.

Detail · 05 — FAQ

Questions we get every week.

If something below isn't covered, the docs are exhaustive — and the team responds inside an hour on Slack Connect.

Which models are supported?

chevron_right

Llama 3 (8B + 70B), Mistral 7B, Mixtral 8x22B, Gemma 2, Phi-3, and Stable Diffusion XL out of the box. Custom GGUF and safetensor weights can be uploaded from the dashboard or via ec push.

How is cold-start handled?

chevron_right

Snapshot-restore, not container boot. We freeze a runtime's memory state once warm and resurrect it on demand in <5ms — even after hours of idle. There's no penalty for low-traffic endpoints.

Where are inferences run?

chevron_right

Across 12 regions: IAD, ORD, LAX, GRU, DUB, LHR, FRA, AMS, NRT, SYD, SIN, BOM. You can pin a deployment to a region pool (e.g. eu-only) for data-residency compliance.

What's the rate limit?

chevron_right

Free tier: 60 RPM. Pro: 600 RPM, burstable to 2,400 for 60 seconds. Enterprise: uncapped against a committed-throughput contract — typical bookings sit between 50–500 RPS sustained.

Do you offer enterprise SLAs?

chevron_right

Yes — 99.99% uptime, p99 latency budgets per route, named incident commander, 15-minute RTO, and quarterly business-review syncs. SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 reports under NDA.

Is my data retained?

chevron_right

No prompts, completions, or tool calls are persisted by default. Logging is opt-in per project, scoped to a 14-day rolling window, and stored in your selected region. We are never training on your inputs.

Can I bring my own model weights?

chevron_right

Yes. Push a GGUF, safetensor, or ONNX bundle via ec push ./weights. We compile to our runtime, sign the artefact, and replicate it across the region pool you select. Typical onboarding is under 4 minutes.
