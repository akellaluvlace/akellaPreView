The Seven Sketch Commandments.

Bent twice. Broken thrice. Re-pinned every Sunday.

  1. I.

    Open the page before the laptop.

    If the keyboard wins the first five minutes, the day belongs to the inbox.

    cmd_01
  2. II.

    A bad doodle beats a perfect outline.

    The wobble carries the meaning. Tidiness costs ideas.

    cmd_02
  3. III.

    Keep the eraser in the drawer.

    Cross out, don't disappear. The crossed-out version is half the audit trail.

    cmd_03
  4. IV.

    One arrow per intention.

    Three arrows means three ideas; do not let them merge into one undecided fork.

    cmd_04
  5. V.

    Date every page in the corner.

    Future-you will need it. The undated page is gravity-free; nothing sticks.

    cmd_05
  6. VI.

    Highlighter last.

    Yellow on top of pen reads as confidence. Yellow on top of pencil reads as panic.

    cmd_06
  7. VII.

    Close the book on the hour.

    Sketching past the bell ruins the next page. Tape it, photograph it, walk away.

    cmd_07
