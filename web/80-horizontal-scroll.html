— Manifesto · 04

Five working notes pinned above the table.

  1. I

    Begin in plan.

    Section first. Renderings second. The plan tells you whether it deserves the photograph.

    [ tenet_01 ]
  2. II

    Negative space is structure.

    The walls you choose not to draw carry the building. Every project gets at least one carved-out room.

    [ tenet_02 ]
  3. III

    Choose a single material.

    Concrete or oak or limestone. Pick one, repeat it through the volume; the second material is the lighting.

    [ tenet_03 ]
  4. IV

    Photograph at four o'clock.

    North-light projects in the morning, south-light projects in the late afternoon. The hour decides the silhouette.

    [ tenet_04 ]
  5. V

    Hand-fold the spec book.

    Print the bind. Cut the paper. The client meets the studio in the binding before they meet the building.

    [ tenet_05 ]

— Atelier · 06

A working room, fourteen years.

Format Studio occupies the second floor of a 1928 print works in central Geneva. The drafting table is older than the studio. The plotter is a plotter, not an inkjet. Visitors are admitted by appointment.
