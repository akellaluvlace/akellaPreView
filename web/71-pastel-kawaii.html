— Sweet FAQ

Tiny questions, sugar-free answers

Everything you wanted to know about your next mochi delivery.

How long does shipping take?

+

We hand-pack and ship every order within 24 hours, chilled. Most boxes arrive in 1–3 days within the country, kept cool with our reusable little ice-pillows.

Are the mochi really gluten-free?

+

Yes! Our dough is made from 100% sweet rice flour (mochiko) — naturally gluten-free. We prep in a dedicated workspace to keep things friendly for sensitive tummies.

How do I store them once they arrive?

+

Pop them in the fridge and enjoy within 5 days for peak fluff. For longer storage, freeze them — let each piece rest at room temp for 8–10 minutes before nibbling.

Can I customise a gift box?

+

Absolutely. Pick your 9 or 16 favorite mochi, add a handwritten note, and choose from three pastel ribbon colors. Birthdays, just-becauses, and apology gifts welcome.

Do you do subscriptions?

+

Yes — the Mochi Club ships a surprise seasonal box every two or four weeks, with two flavors that never appear on the regular menu. Pause anytime, no awkward emails.
