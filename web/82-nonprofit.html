IV — Approach

Five disciplines, one ocean.

"We don't parachute in. Every program runs for a minimum decade because reefs heal on geological time, not grant cycles."

  1. 01

    Long-term Monitoring

    Acoustic arrays, photo-quadrats, and satellite tagging across decade-long baselines.

    Active since 2011 · 240 sites
  2. 02

    Community Co-Management

    Marine protected areas designed and stewarded with the villages whose livelihoods depend on them.

    14 MPAs · 38 villages
  3. 03

    Habitat Restoration

    Coral micro-fragmentation, mangrove planting, and seagrass replanting at scale.

    2.1M corals · 9,400 ha mangrove
  4. 04

    Policy Translation

    Field data turned into legal protections — fishing-zone treaties, vessel speed limits, plastic bans.

    31 statutes informed
  5. 05

    Next Generation

    Field scholarships for young scientists from coastal communities — paid, mentored, published.

    62 scholars · 19 PhDs

VI — Questions

Frequently asked.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

chevron_right

Yes. The Bluefield Fund is a registered 501(c)(3) public charity (EIN #84-1234567). U.S. donors receive a deductible-gift acknowledgment for every contribution above $25. International donors should consult our partner network in the EU, UK, and Australia.

How is field-research funding allocated?

chevron_right

Sixty-two cents of every dollar funds direct field research — staff scientists, instrument arrays, vessel time, and station operations. The full breakdown is published in Section V above and audited annually by an independent firm.

Can I volunteer in the field?

chevron_right

Field placements run 8–12 weeks and require open-water certification plus a science or community-engagement background. We open eight slots a year, prioritizing applicants from coastal communities adjacent to our partner sites.

Do you accept crypto, stock, or DAF gifts?

chevron_right

Yes — appreciated stock, donor-advised-fund grants, and cryptocurrency (BTC, ETH, USDC) are all accepted. Stock and DAF transfers are processed by our partner foundation; please email development@bluefieldfund.org for routing.

Where do I find your published research?

chevron_right

Field reports, peer-reviewed papers, and open datasets are published quarterly under a CC-BY license. The full archive — 184 reports as of FY24 — is mirrored on our Field Reports page and on the Open Science Framework.
