Frequently
Transmitted.

Are pieces made by hand?

Every Chrome_Hearts artifact is poured, filed, and polished by a small atelier of six. The vacuum cooling and oxidation steps are computer-monitored; everything else is touch.

What is the .925 standard?

.925 indicates 92.5% pure silver alloyed with 7.5% copper for structural memory. Our oxidized finish is a chemical reaction with the copper, not a coating — it cannot wear off.

How do I care for liquid finishes?

Wipe with a dry microfiber after wear. Do not use chemical polishers — they remove the patina that gives oxidized pieces their depth. The mirror finish self-restores in three to five wears.

Is metavault delivery covered?

Every artifact ships with both a physical certificate (engraved alloy plate) and a metavault token. Token storage is included for the lifetime of the wearer's wallet.

Can pieces be resized?

Yes. Our adaptive pieces flex within a 1.5-size range without reworking. For permanent resizing, send to Atelier 07 — the work is free for original owners across the lifetime of the artifact.

Where can I see them in person?

By appointment in Tokyo (Sector 7), Paris (4e), and the metavault. Address & coordinates are released to confirmed appointment holders 24 hours prior.
