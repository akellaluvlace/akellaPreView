V / DOCTRINE

Four vows held
by the house

Carried in the front of every contract, kept by every hand that signs.

I lock

Discretion

We keep no public client list. No name appears in this archive without an invitation we drafted, signed, and sealed in wax. The houses we serve do not need our advertisement, and we, in turn, do not need theirs.

ADOPTED MMXIV · NO REVISIONS
II verified

Provenance

Every piece is hand-signed at completion and entered, by hand, into the studio ledger. Each entry carries the maker, the hour, and the room in which it was finished. The ledger remains, and the work is traceable to its first breath.

ADOPTED MMXVI · NO REVISIONS
OBSERVED
III auto_awesome

Patience

We work in long windows, never in short ones. A commission begun in autumn is delivered in spring, and a commission begun in spring waits for the next autumn. Haste makes a different object — usually a worse one — and we have no quarrel with the seasons.

ADOPTED MMXVII · NO REVISIONS
IV nightlight

Quietude

The studio runs on candle and lamp from dusk to first light. No music in the rooms where finishing is done; no voices above a whisper near the press. Quiet is the medium we work in — louder than any tool, and the only one we cannot replace.

V / THE MANIFESTO

Five articles
of the craft

— A small ledger, written by the lamp.

These articles are kept in the front of every contract we sign. They are old measures, written by hands now stilled, and they hold.

format_quote
"A craft kept by indifference to applause is the only craft that survives the applause."
— Studio Note · 014
  1. I

    Of the Material

    Matter resists. The grain refuses, the colour drifts, the seam splits. We treat resistance as instruction, not failure.

    — ARTICLE · I
  2. II

    Of the Hour

    The hour at which a piece is begun is encoded in its body. We work between the wolf hour and the second cock — the time the world has stopped speaking.

    — ARTICLE · II
  3. III

    Of Concealment

    What is most precious is buried. Visibility is a tax we charge sparingly, and never to the loudest bidder.

    — ARTICLE · III
  4. IV

    Of the Witness

    Craft endures by indifference to its audience. We make for the eye that arrives after the speaker has gone silent.

    — ARTICLE · IV
  5. V

    Of Transmutation

    Each piece is a small refusal of decay — a vessel made to hold what time, otherwise, would erase.

    — ARTICLE · V
