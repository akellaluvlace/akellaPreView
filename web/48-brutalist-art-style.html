// DOCTRINE

FIVE TENETS OF THE PROGRAMME

Editorial position drafted at the founding of UNIT 07. Reissued annually. Read aloud at the opening of each season.

"FORM IS A SYMPTOM OF THE STRUCTURE BEHIND IT. WE STUDY THE SYMPTOM." — FOUNDING NOTE · 09.1994
  1. I

    RAWNESS OVER POLISH

    Material is not a metaphor. Concrete is concrete. A circuit board is a circuit board. The work is not allowed to forget what it is made of.

    [ TENET_01 ]
  2. II

    SYSTEM BEFORE STYLE

    Every exhibition begins as a constraint, not a moodboard. Style is what is left after the system has been satisfied.

    [ TENET_02 ]
  3. III

    DOCUMENT EVERYTHING

    If it was not photographed, measured, and indexed, it did not happen. The archive outlives the exhibition.

    [ TENET_03 ]
  4. IV

    DECAY IS A MEDIUM

    Things that fall apart in public are more honest than things that do not. We refuse to seal what was meant to weather.

    [ TENET_04 ]
  5. V

    REFUSE THE SOFT EXIT

    No fade. No swell. End where the work ends. The viewer leaves the building with the same nervous system they arrived in.

    [ TENET_05 ]
