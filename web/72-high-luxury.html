Compact · I

Geneva-only service.

Movements return to the Geneva atelier on a fifteen-year cycle. Disassembly, ultrasonic cleaning, regulation — performed by the same watchmakers who built the calibre. Cost is sealed at allocation.

I.

Are pieces sold online?

+

Numbered editions are placed by appointment only — Geneva, Milan, Tokyo, New York. Heritage references are reserved for clients of the inner circle.

II.

What does the warranty cover?

+

Eight years on movement, lifetime on case anglage. Service is performed only at the Geneva atelier — we collect, restore, return.

III.

Can I commission a bespoke?

+

A studio of three accepts six commissions per annum. Conversations begin with a private viewing; the first calibre is delivered fourteen months later.

IV.

How do I authenticate a heritage piece?

+

Each heritage reference carries a hidden signature beneath the rotor. Ship the piece in its original box; we examine, certify, and return within three weeks.

V.

Where is the boutique?

+

An unmarked door on Rue du Rhône. The address is given to clients only. The bell is answered between 14:00 and 18:00, weekdays.
