— II · Atelier Manifesto

Three tenets, kept in private.

A small studio's working principles, written once and rarely revisited.

  1. I

    Restraint over ornament.

    Every seam earns its place. Garments are drafted to read at six paces and be felt at one — not the other way around.

    [ TENET_01 · KEPT ]
  2. II

    Materials before mood.

    Wool, silk, leather. Sourced quietly from three mills the founder has worked with for fourteen years. Mood follows the cloth, not the other way around.

    [ TENET_02 · KEPT ]
  3. III

    Slowness is a method.

    Two collections a year. No drops, no capsules, no surprise releases. The work moves at the speed of the room it was made in.

    [ TENET_03 · KEPT ]
