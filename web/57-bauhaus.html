Four standing principles
P · 01

Geometry first

Every form distilled to circle, square, triangle. Ornament is the residue of indecision.

Industry honest

Tubular steel left tubular. Welds shown. The means of making is the finish.

04 Heritage

One Hundred
+ Five Years.

  1. 1919 Walter Gropius founds the Staatliches Bauhaus in Weimar. Manifesto: Art into Industry.
  2. 1925 School relocates to Dessau. Marcel Breuer designs the Wassily Chair — first commercial use of tubular steel.
  3. 1933 Bauhaus closes under political pressure. Disciples emigrate — the ideas spread to Chicago, Tel Aviv, Ulm.
  4. 2024 WERKSTATT carries the principles forward. Same forms, same discipline, no nostalgia.

06 What Each Piece Carries

STANDING
GUARANTEES.

Every piece that leaves the Werkstatt carries four written promises. They are stamped on the foot, mirrored in our ledger, and honoured for the life of the object — not the life of a returns policy.

Steel. Oak. Eisengarn. Brass. Each material is sourced for a reason and warranted on its own terms — but the four guarantees below are universal.

From the bench-log · entry 0184

"A chair is finished when it is still standing in fifty years — not when it ships."

— Karl Mayer · Master draughtsman · 2024

Fifty-year structural warranty

G · 01

Frame, joinery, weld, foot — anything that carries weight, we carry for fifty years. Cracked tube, bent mitre, sagging Eisengarn: returned, repaired, sent back.

Repair for postage

G · 02

Send the broken piece. We mend the joint, replace the cushion, re-weld the frame. Postage one way; no labour charged. The bench is open while the kettle is on.

Every piece numbered

G · 03

Stamped on the foot: edition, year, draughtsman, bench. Trace any piece back to the day it left the workshop — clay batch, weld run, fabric roll. Provenance is structural.

Draughtsman's certificate

G · 04

A signed sheet ships with each piece — the drawing set, the materials, the weight, the firing curve. Filed with you, mirrored in our ledger. Insurable, willable, lendable.
