04 Heritage

One Hundred
+ Five Years.

  1. 1919 Walter Gropius founds the Staatliches Bauhaus in Weimar. Manifesto: Art into Industry.
  2. 1925 School relocates to Dessau. Marcel Breuer designs the Wassily Chair — first commercial use of tubular steel.
  3. 1933 Bauhaus closes under political pressure. Disciples emigrate — the ideas spread to Chicago, Tel Aviv, Ulm.
  4. 2024 WERKSTATT carries the principles forward. Same forms, same discipline, no nostalgia.
