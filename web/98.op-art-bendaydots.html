Four panels we live by

PANEL · 01

Creator-owned, always.

No work-for-hire. No moral-rights waivers. Every page in the catalog still belongs to the artist who drew it — they take 70% on the issue, 100% on the IP.

PANEL · 02

Print on stock that lasts.

120 gsm uncoated, soy ink, glue-spine — every issue is built to outlive the website. Reprints are open until the run sells out, then the plate is destroyed.

/// CHAPTER A · STUDIO ///

Drawn at the big table.

Every cover, every interior page, every back-matter ad — ruled and inked at one long oak table on the third floor of the warehouse. No Figma boards, no AI assists, no remote pen-pals.

Light from the north window. Bristol stock by Strathmore. Ink by Speedball. Music kept to one pair of headphones at a time.

  • Table · oak · 3.6 mSince 2017
  • Stock · Bristol 270 gsmStrathmore
  • Headphones · 1 pairNo exceptions
/// CHAPTER B · PRESS ///

Printed at midnight.

Two-colour offset on a 1976 Heidelberg, run by a printer who has been on that machine since 1989. The press starts at 22:00, finishes by 04:00 — that's the whole month's run, every month.

No digital print, no print-on-demand. The screw, the rubber blanket, the ink-fountain — the same ones that printed our first issue in 2017.
