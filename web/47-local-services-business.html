— VIII · Frequently asked

Things people ask before booking

call 01 234 5678

Are you fully insured for work over my property?

add

Yes — €6.5 m public liability and €13 m employer's liability cover, with current certificates from AXA. We email both before any job starts; many heritage and council clients require them on file.

My tree might be protected — what do I do?

add

Don't lift a saw. Send us the address and we'll check the council TPO register and conservation-area mapping for free. If a permit is needed, we draft and submit it on your behalf — average 4-week turnaround.

Do you do emergency work outside office hours?

add

Yes, 24 hours, 365 days, across Dublin city and county. Average on-site time is 92 minutes. Storm-Éowyn week (Jan 2025) we ran 71 call-outs in 4 days; ring 087 123 4567 day or night.

What happens to the green waste?

add

All chip goes to Bord na Móna's compost facility in Kildare. Larger logs are split for our firewood programme — we donate ~30 cubic metres a year to community groups in Dublin 1, 7 and 8.

What's the lead time for non-emergency work?

add

5–10 working days for surgery and clearance, 3–7 days for hedge work. We hold one short-notice slot every Friday for time-critical jobs that aren't emergencies — sale completions, insurance assessments, and the like.
