Chapter IV — Inside the Playbook

A craft you can see being built.

Three workrooms. Each one a fixed habit, a fixed deliverable, and a fixed week to ship it. Not a content drip — an editorial studio.

The Vault — find what people will actually pay for.

Forty-eight hours of niche pressure-testing. We write five candidate angles by hand, run them past a small list, then choose the one with three paying signals before you ever build the page.

  • 01Niche-pressure interview script (12 questions)
  • 02The "three paying signals" rubric
  • 03Validation worksheet — fill in 1 hour
The Press — write the issue that converts.

A six-week shaping room: the cold-open, the pull-quote, the close. Every issue you draft gets line-edited against a single conversion question — would a stranger pay rent for this paragraph?

"The Press is where I stopped trying to sound clever and started getting paid."— Lin Toh, Cohort 04
The Bindery — package, price, and renew.

The last four weeks. We ship a tiered pricing page, a year-one renewal letter, and a deliberate way to retire issues into a back-catalogue product. The number on the door becomes a number in the bank.

Chapter VIII — Common questions

Before you join.

Do I need an existing audience?

expand_more

No. Half of last year's cohort started from a list of zero. The Vault week is built for that case — you'll have a 250-person waitlist by week three or your tuition back.

How much time per week is realistic?

expand_more

Six hours. Three for the workroom, three for shaping your own draft. Most operators do it on Tuesday and Saturday mornings.

What if my niche is "too small"?

expand_more

A 1,200-person paid wine letter outearns most 80,000-person free ones. Small niches with sharp pricing are the entire point. The Vault rubric is built to find them.

Do you teach a specific platform — Substack, Beehiiv, ghost?

expand_more

Platform-agnostic. The Bindery section ships templates for the three majors plus a self-hosted recipe. Switch later and the writing still works.

Is there a refund window?

expand_more

Fourteen days, no questions. After the Vault week — if you're not on a paying-signal trajectory — write us and we'll send the tuition back the same day.
