// THE OUTPUT · 04 PHASES

FROM BRIEF TO BLAST.

Every engagement runs the same four phases. 12 weeks, no extensions. The pipeline is the deliverable.

  1. hearing
    01 Wks 01–02

    Listen.

    Two-week deep-dive. Founders, ops, customer interviews, competitive teardown. We finish with a list of three uncomfortable truths.

    → Truth deck arrow_forward
  2. draw
    02 Wks 03–05

    Sketch.

    Three opposing routes drawn in parallel. We refuse to converge early. Every route gets killed in the room before one survives.

    → 3 routes arrow_forward
  3. construction
    03 Wks 06–10

    Forge.

    Brand system, voice, motion, code, and the launch site. Built in the open with the client team in our Figma all day.

    → Live build arrow_forward
  4. rocket_launch
    04 Wks 11–12

    Ship.

    Hard launch. Press kit, asset library, governance handoff. We disappear at week 13. Your team owns the brand from day one.

    → Hand-off

// BEDROCK · 04 NON-NEGOTIABLES

Why brands keep coming back.

The four things that built our reputation. Picked apart, hardened, then carved into how the studio actually runs.

Lead pillar
I verified

Senior team. Full stop.

Every project is led by a partner who's shipped at least a hundred brands. No interns hidden in the org chart, no junior strategists rehearsing on your dime, no bait-and-switch after the SOW signs. The names in the kick-off deck do every line of work, every week, until launch.

Avg 12 yrs in seat arrow_forward
II design_services

In-house craft.

Strategy, design, motion, code — all under one roof in Berlin and Tokyo. Nothing brokered to a downstream studio you've never met. The hand that draws the wordmark also writes the manifesto, also names the colour, also commits the front-end. The work stays coherent because the same people stay accountable.

Zero subcontractors arrow_forward
III handshake

Honest pricing.

One number, posted up-front the day we agree to engage. Not an estimate, not a range, not a ladder of optional add-ons. Twelve weeks of senior work. The price doesn't move because you said something interesting in week six. If the brief grows, we tell you the day it grows — and we tell you why.

No change orders arrow_forward
IV bolt

Long-after partners.

We disappear at week 13, then we pick up the phone every quarter for the next decade. The brand belongs to your team — the institutional memory of every decision, every kill, every reason behind a glyph stays here, on tap, free, for as long as the brand lives. Most studios bill the hand-off. We bill the work.
