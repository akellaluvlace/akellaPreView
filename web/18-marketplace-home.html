— 06 · Questions

Things collectors ask

Everything about provenance, shipping, returns and supporting the studios you buy from.

Speak to a curator arrow_forward

Are all the works original?

expand_more

Yes. Every painting, sculpture, and ceramic on Commons is a one-of-one or part of a numbered edition signed by the artist. Each piece ships with a certificate of authenticity.

How does shipping work internationally?

expand_more

Studios pack and crate from their own workspace. We coordinate fully insured pickup, customs paperwork, and door-to-door tracking — typically 5–14 working days depending on origin.

Can I return a piece if it doesn't suit the room?

expand_more

14-day collector returns are standard. Funds stay in escrow until you confirm — if you change your mind, the studio receives the work back in its original crating with shipping covered by us.

How much of the price reaches the artist?

expand_more

82% of the listed price goes directly to the studio. The remaining 18% covers payment processing, escrow, customs handling, and the curatorial team. No hidden buyer's premium.

Do you commission new work?

expand_more

A handful of our studios accept private commissions. Reach out through the curator inbox with your reference imagery and timeline — we'll only forward briefs the studios are likely to accept.
