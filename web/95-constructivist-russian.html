// MANIFESTO · V TENETS · 1924–2026

ART
IS NOT
A SERVICE.

  1. I

    REFUSE THE LOBBY.

    No champagne, no plaque, no donor wall. The room you walk into is the work.

  2. II

    BUILD WITH STEEL.

    Sets are welded, not painted. Costumes outlive the run. Posters survive the building.

  3. III

    PAY THE ENSEMBLE.

    Equity scale, every act. Profit-share if there's profit. The collective is the asset.

  4. IV

    PROVOKE / DON'T COMFORT.

    If everyone leaves agreeing, we have failed. The seat exists to be uncomfortable.

  5. V

    THE STAGE IS A WEAPON. THE AUDIENCE IS THE TARGET.

    Every show is loaded before the doors open. We will not apologise for the recoil.

— ДОКТРИНЫ · IV PROMISES ·

FOUR DOCTRINES / NO ALTERNATIVES.

Drawn up in 1924, redrawn this season. Each doctrine is enforced by the collective, not the box office.

I precision_manufacturing

PRAGMATISM / NO POETRY ABOUT BUDGET.

A scene that demands a thousand candles gets thirty. A scene that demands one truck gets the truck. Means dictate metaphor; metaphor never dictates means. We name our fixed costs out loud, on stage, in the prologue, before any actor speaks.

DOCTRINE_I arrow_forward
ZERO ALTERNATIVE
II shield

DISCIPLINE / THE CALL IS 17:00.

Late is absent. Absent is replaced. Replaced is recorded. The collective survives because the schedule survives — there is no actor whose name protects them from the iron rule of the call sheet. We have built nothing that we are not prepared to lose.

DOCTRINE_II arrow_forward
III psychology

REFUSAL / WE TURN DOWN MORE THAN WE TAKE.

Three commissions a season, on average, are declined. Banks, party machines, weapons firms, novelty branding tie-ups. The work that survives is the work we wanted to make; the work we refused is the proof that the survivors are not for sale.

DOCTRINE_III arrow_forward
IV verified

CONTINUITY / EVERY ACT IS A SEQUEL.

No production is invented from nothing. Every show inherits a prop, a costume, a line, a wound from the show before it. The continuity is not nostalgia — it is the receipt that proves the collective existed before today and intends to exist after.
