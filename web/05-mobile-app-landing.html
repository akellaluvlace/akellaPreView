— VI · Questions

Asked at twilight.

Is my journal private?

expand_more

Entries are end-to-end encrypted on device, then synced via your iCloud or Google account. We never see your text, and there's no analytics on the contents of your entries — only on whether the app launched.

Will it nag me with streaks?

expand_more

No streaks, no badges, no red dots. The only nudge is a single optional sunset reminder that you choose the time of. Skipping a week is part of the rhythm too.

Can I export my entries?

expand_more

Yes — Markdown, plain text, or a printable PDF photobook with mood colours preserved as page tints. Your archive belongs to you, on your terms.

How much does Sundial cost?

expand_more

Free for one entry per day with the standard mood palette. Sundial Plus unlocks unlimited entries, the timeline view, and the sunset photobook export — $4 a month or $36 a year.

Does it work offline?

expand_more

Completely. Write on a plane, in a tent, on a sleeper train. Entries sync the next time you reconnect. No connection required for the editor, the mood slider, or the timeline.
