— Our Approach

Four movements, one engagement.

Refined across 38 institutional engagements. Every movement is iterative, evidence-led, and signed off jointly with the executive team.

  1. I Wks 01–06

    Diagnose

    Six weeks of unscripted interviews across three organisational layers. We read the room before we read the org chart.

    Listen → Map →
  2. II Wks 06–14

    Architect

    A target-state blueprint for the system, the data, and the people. Drafted with the executive team, never imposed on it.

    Model → Decide →
  3. III Wks 14–22

    Deploy

    Phased rollout against falsifiable claims. Live telemetry against the kill criteria from movement II. No "big bang" cutovers.

    Pilot → Scale →
  4. IV Wks 22–26

    Sustain

    A maintenance protocol and a closed-form report. We withdraw at month nine. The institution owns the system, not us.

    Hand-off → Withdraw →
