Reasons · Sufficient

Hours of the House

Four standing reasons to abandon the daylight world. Each accompanied by its own particular libation.

No · I

The Late Hour

When the avenues quiet and the gas-lamps narrow their flame — that is when the bar is at its most generous. Doors close at three; conversations rarely do.

11 PM — 3 AM Old Fashioned · neat
No · II

The Trio Set

Three musicians, one ribbon mic, no amplifier. Bass, brush, vocal — five sets a week, no two alike. The room arranges itself around them by 9.30.

Tue — Sat · 21:30 French 75 · pre-set
No · III

The Apothecary

Eighty-four bottles behind the brass rail. Twelve infusions, six bitters, three vermouths house-made and rotated by season. The list is read aloud, never printed.

Master · 11 yrs Negroni Sbagliato
No · IV

The Members' Door

Two reservations a week held back for those who know to ask. The list is kept in a calf-leather book, written in fountain pen. Your name, once entered, stays.

By introduction Bottle service · ask
