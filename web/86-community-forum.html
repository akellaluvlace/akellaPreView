[ Why members stay · 04 pillars ]

Built like a private kitchen.

Four pillars hold it up. Each one is the answer to a complaint we had about every other forum we ever loved and quit.

Lead pillar
I. verified

Quality threads, by design.

Every thread starts with a stake — a question, a draft, a problem worth fifteen minutes. Replies that aren't useful get folded under a "more" tab. The signal stays loud because the noise has nowhere to live.

Curated daily arrow_forward
II. shield

No-noise policy, signed at the door.

No "first!" replies. No screenshot dunks. No outrage threads about other forums. The four-line code-of-conduct is short on purpose — every member ticks it on the way in and three moderators read every flag.

3 strikes · 0 saved arrow_forward
III. support_agent

Senior moderators, paid.

Three people are paid to read every thread on a rotating schedule — a working producer, a touring drummer, a label A&R. They reply to feedback within the day and they vote on every member who applies.

Mods on call · 09—21 GMT arrow_forward
IV. psychology

Real-name optional, vouching mandatory.

Pick any handle you like. The only thing required is a vouch from a member already inside, and a single line on what you're working on. Anonymity is fine. Hiding the work isn't — that's how trust gets built.

Where the builders gather.

[ How it feels · 02 ]

Find your people. Quietly.

Members come from twenty-eight countries and most of them never post on the open internet. The reason is the same in every interview we run — they wanted somewhere small enough to remember the names of the people they were talking to, and big enough that someone always had a useful answer.

Why we built it like a private kitchen, not a stadium.
