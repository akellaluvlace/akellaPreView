II — How to use

A 45-minute ritual.

Drift was made to slot inside an unhurried evening. Four small acts that lower the room's temperature before the body lets go.

  1. 01

    Dim, then settle.

    Lower the room to candle-grade light by 21:30. Phones face-down, screens out of arm's reach.

  2. 02

    Two droppers, held.

    Place 1 mL under the tongue and hold for 30 seconds before swallowing. The botanicals enter cleanly through sublingual capillaries.

  3. 03

    Twenty quiet minutes.

    Read a page, stretch, or sit with tea. The valerian and L-theanine begin to elevate GABA tone within fifteen minutes.

  4. 04

    Released into sleep.

    By 22:15 the body recognises the cue. Magnesium glycinate eases muscle tension; cortisol falls without sedation.

Methodology

Subjects were stratified by baseline cortisol AUC and assigned across two 28-day arms with a 14-day washout. Sleep latency, wake-after-sleep-onset, and morning self-reported alertness were primary outcomes; texture and skin-barrier integrity were secondary.
