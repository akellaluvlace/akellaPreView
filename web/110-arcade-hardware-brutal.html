Operator's Doctrine

SEC. 04 // PROTOCOL

— Four standing protocols. Each module ships with the doctrine printed on the rear panel — not in the manual.

PROT · 01
power

Cold Boot, Daily

Power-cycle every twenty-four hours. Capacitors prefer rest, the bench-log prefers timestamps, the operator prefers silence at 03:00 sharp.

Cycle · 24h 220V triple-phase
PROT · 02
memory

Audit Every Pin

Visual inspection of every solder joint, every contact, every cap before the rack is closed. The probe is a microscope. The note is in graphite.

Tolerance · ±0.5% Mil-spec only
PROT · 03
tune

Calibrate Cold

Voltage, current, frequency — calibrated against a sealed reference once per quarter. The reference is older than the bench. The bench respects it.

Drift · <3ppm Quarterly
PROT · 04
archive

Log Or Lose It

Every action ledgered: time, hand, current draw, anomaly. The book has been kept for fourteen years; nothing leaves the bench undocumented.

// CHAPTER A · THE CONSOLE

Ribbon, tape, fader.

The console begins where the carrier ends. Magnetic tape, cold ferrite, three-band parametric — every signal that leaves The Nexus passes through analog before the operator signs off.

No DSP shortcut. No "vintage modeling" plug-in. The hum on the master is real hum, the saturation is real iron in the transformer, and the warmth is the price of admission.

// CHAPTER B · THE BENCH

Solder, scope, stock.

Twenty-four feet of pegboard. Every component in its drawer. The bench is open from nine until the kettle is empty — usually around midnight, sometimes later if the trace is unfinished.

If we don't have the part, we wind it. If we don't have the tool, we make it. The shelf above the iron carries thirty-two notebooks of every repair logged since the founding.
