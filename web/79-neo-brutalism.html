How fast can you actually ship?

Most launches land in 6-10 weeks. Tight scope can hit 5 days. We refuse projects we cannot ship without losing the edge — fast is a constraint, not a feature.

Do you take retainers?

Yes — capped at three at a time so we stay senior on every account. Retainers are billed monthly and scoped to a clear roadmap. No babysitting.

Will you work with our internal team?

Always. We embed in your Slack, run weekly demos, and document everything we ship in a handover doc your team owns. No black boxes.

What is your minimum project?

Identity sprints from $14k. Web builds from $32k. Launch campaigns scoped per channel. We send a fixed quote within 48h of the brief.

Where are you based?

HQ in Seoul. The studio runs across three time zones — Asia, Europe, and the Americas — so handoffs cross calendars cleanly. We ship 24/7 without all-nighters.
