— III · Provenance

A practice of careful keeping.

Founded in a converted greenhouse on the edge of the Devon hills, Cabinet & Seed has spent four generations preserving heirloom varietals that the modern catalogue has discarded. Every accession is hand-pollinated, hand-cleaned, and hand-packeted under the supervision of a single curator.

"We do not grow seed. We keep it — quietly, for the next gardener."

— Edith Halsworth, Curator IV
