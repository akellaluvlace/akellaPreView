About Ren

Ren Park is a media operator and strategist building tools and frameworks for the next generation of independent creators. After spending a decade leading editorial strategy at major publications, he transitioned to building a solo media business that now reaches over 200,000 professionals weekly.

Through his newsletter, videos, and courses, Ren documents the operational realities of building a digital business. His work focuses on sustainable growth, uncoupling time from income, and creating high-leverage digital assets. He currently resides in Portland, Oregon.
