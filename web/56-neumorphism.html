— Asked · 09

Things people ask before they buy

Answers we kept short. Anything else, the studio replies in a day.

Photographed in a SmartLiving home in Lisbon. The hub sits behind that pile of books, somewhere.

Do I need to rewire anything?

No. Sensors are wireless and the hub plugs into any outlet. The thermostat is the only thing that touches a wire, and it's a five-minute swap.

Will it work without internet?

Locally, yes. The hub keeps your routines in memory and the sensors talk to it directly. Cloud features (remote control, weather routines) pause until you're back online.

What about the data — who sees it?

You. Routines are processed on the hub. We get aggregate uptime stats and crash reports, never room-by-room behaviour. There's a switch to turn even that off.

Does it talk to other devices?

Matter, Thread, HomeKit, Google Home. We avoid platform fights — if your speakers and bulbs are already in someone else's app, we'll join, not replace.

What's the warranty?

Five years on the hub, two on each sensor. We repair before we replace; mail it back, we send it back fixed in roughly a week.
