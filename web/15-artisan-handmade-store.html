The Wheel Room

— II · Origins

A practice rooted in slowness.

Clay Journal began in a converted boathouse on Cardigan Bay in 2014 — one wheel, one kiln, one decision: never to scale beyond what two hands could finish in a season.

Each vessel passes through eleven stages over thirty days. We do not glaze on Mondays. We do not fire when the wind crosses 25 knots. The kiln, like the work, asks for patience.
