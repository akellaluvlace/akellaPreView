Each Monolith leaves Lisbon with a hand-stamped serial and a single signature on the underside.

A.01 — Aluminum 6061-T6 · 1.8 KG

CNC-milled from a single block of aircraft-grade aluminum, then sand-blasted and bead-textured at five distinct grit levels for a finish that catches light, not fingerprints.

A.02 — Switches 45 G · 24 H BURN-IN

Custom Monolith Linear stems wound to 45-gram actuation. Each switch is hand-lubed with Krytox 205G0, factory-soldered, and burned-in for a full day before assembly.

A.03 — Voicing 22 DB · 380 HZ FLOOR

Three layers of internal foam — Poron, EVA, silicone — engineered to flatten resonance below 380 Hz. Net acoustic signature: 22 dB at typing distance, full-range, room-tone.
